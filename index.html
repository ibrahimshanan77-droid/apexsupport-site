Questions store owners ask

Where are your agents located?

Our team works remotely from Beirut and Syria. Agents write fluent English with an American-style tone and work your hours, including your nights, Monday to Saturday.

We tried outsourcing before and it went badly. Why would this be different?

A common cause is shared agents juggling many brands from a script. You get one dedicated agent who learns your store, works in your helpdesk where you can see every reply, and you talk to me directly when something's off.

Which helpdesks do you work in?

We work in Zendesk, Gorgias, Zoho Desk, Re:amaze and most other helpdesks. Tell me which one you have before we start and I'll confirm we can work in it during the call.

Can I see what your agents send?

Yes. Every reply goes out from your own helpdesk, so you can read, review or edit any of them, and you can remove access at any time.

What if an agent is sick or it's a holiday?

We cover the absence with a trained replacement agent where we can, and tell you in advance about planned days off. We work Monday to Saturday.

Is my store data safe?

You give us a user seat in your helpdesk, with the permissions you choose. We never collect card numbers or passwords from customers, and you can remove access at any time.

What happens after I send a request?

I reply personally and we book a short call. Then you add our agent to your helpdesk, the agent learns your policies and macros, and your 10-day free trial starts. You judge the replies before you pay anything.

Do I pay extra for the helpdesk seat?

Our agent needs a user seat in your helpdesk. Some helpdesks charge per seat and others do not, so I will tell you before we start what, if anything, it adds to your bill.

How do I pay?

Monthly, by MoneyGram, a licensed international money transfer service. Bank wire is available on request. Our team works from Beirut and Syria, and comprehensive US sanctions on Syria have been lifted. I'll send clear, step-by-step payment instructions with your invoice, so there is nothing for you to figure out.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. It's month-to-month with 30 days' notice to cancel or change the number of agents.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Who we are. Apex Support provides outsourced customer support for online stores. Our team works remotely from Beirut and Syria. You can reach me, Ibrahim Shanan, at ibrahim@apexsupport.co.

What we collect on this website. Only what you type into the request form: your name, work email, store website, and an optional message (which may include your helpdesk or preferred call times). This website does not use advertising cookies or analytics trackers.

How we use it. To reply to your request, schedule a call and set up your free trial. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

Who processes it. Form submissions are handled by Formspree (formspree.io), a form service that delivers them to our email. They are then stored in our email account. If you book a call, we use the details you send for that call only.

Cold email. If you received an email from us, it is because your store's business contact details are publicly listed. Reply "stop" and we will not contact you again.

If you become a client. Our agents work inside your helpdesk using a user seat and the permissions you choose. We do not copy your customers' data out of your helpdesk or store it elsewhere. Confidentiality and data-handling terms will be set out in our service agreement.

How long we keep it. Request-form details are kept while we are in contact with you, and deleted within 12 months if no trial or agreement follows.

Your choices. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing ibrahim@apexsupport.co. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes. If we change this policy we will update the date above.
